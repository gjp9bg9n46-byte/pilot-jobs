What makes the row ours

The four dots are OUT, OFF, ON and IN on one line: grey ends for the gate, navy line for airborne. No other logbook shows taxi time like this. Night is shown as an actual duration, not just an icon, and the flight number and type sit on top like a strip.

Kept from today

Day headers with day totals, +1 on next-day arrivals, the overnight link line (now in the footer), PF/PM, the hh:mm or decimal toggle, and tapping a row to open the flight detail.

Scan review rule

Save is never silently refused. Every blocker shows on the card it belongs to, with a button to resolve it. If anything is left, the bar says exactly how many and where.
