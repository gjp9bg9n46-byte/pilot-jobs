Taken from the Stitch design

Block time large at the top, airport names under the codes, a word under each of OUT, OFF, ON and IN, labelled departure and arrival on the map, great-circle distance, day and night landing chips, a highlighted SIC tile, and an export button.

Left out on purpose

ADS-B, waypoints, flight level, Mach, wind, fuel burn, approach and runway, weather reports, the captain's licence number and any "verified" badge. We don't record any of it, and showing it would be invented.

Calculated, not stored

Distance from the airport coordinates, taxi out and in from OUT/OFF and ON/IN, airborne from OFF to ON. Each one is hidden when the times it needs are missing, never shown as zero.
