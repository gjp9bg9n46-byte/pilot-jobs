What's in this mockup
  1. Header: photo, name in proper capitals, one line "First Officer · Airbus A320 · Cairo, Egypt", key chips, and an Open to work switch. Phone number moves out of the header.
  2. Application readiness (like FlightLog's "OK to fly" check): anything expired, expiring soon or missing, with a coloured dot (red, amber, grey) and a one-tap fix. Airlines check these first.
  3. Snapshot: same hours as the Logbook dashboard (no broken "0 PIC"), plus profile strength that links to the jobs you'd unlock.
  4. One page, no tabs: Licences & ratings (hours on type from your logbook), Medical, Training with next due dates, Right to work & passport, Job preferences (new), Personal details.
  5. Ratings sit under the licence they belong to (like ForeFlight), and a new Checks & recency card holds LPC/OPC and line check dates, which airline recruiters ask for.
  6. Preview as airline shows exactly what a recruiter sees when you apply.
  7. No red bins: each card has one Edit link; deleting happens inside edit.
  8. Licence authority shows the real issuer (ECAA), not "ICAO", so job matching works.
